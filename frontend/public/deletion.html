Data deletion

Last updated November 3, 2026

You can delete everything Stupid holds about you at any time, in either of two ways.

In Stupid

  1. Open Stupid and go to Settings.
  2. Choose Disconnect and delete my data, then confirm.

In Facebook

  1. In Facebook, open Settings & privacy, then Settings, then Apps and websites.
  2. Find Stupid and choose Remove.

Meta then tells Stupid to delete your data, and gives you a confirmation code you can use to check its status.

What is deleted

Your Meta access tokens, your messages and comments, your account choices, your saved AI key, your agent results and your sessions. Nothing is kept afterwards.
