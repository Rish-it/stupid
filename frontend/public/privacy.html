Stupid

Privacy

Last updated November 4, 2026

Stupid helps creators and small businesses read and answer their Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp activity in one place. This page says what Stupid reads, why, where it goes and how to delete it.

What Stupid reads

Only the accounts you pick in Meta's window when you sign in, and only while they are switched on in Stupid:

  • Your name from Facebook, to greet you.
  • Messages to your Facebook Page (Messenger).
  • Instagram direct messages and comments on your posts.
  • WhatsApp messages to your business number.
  • Instagram insights: reach, followers and how your posts did.
  • Ad results: spend, clicks and reach for your ad accounts.

Why

To show them to you in plain words, and to let you answer from Stupid. When you run an agent, Stupid also uses them to draft replies, post ideas and ad advice for you to review. Nothing is sent, posted or changed until you tap.

Where it is kept

On Stupid's server.

AI drafting

Stupid's agents run on Muse Spark Open, an open model from Meta, hosted by our AI provider Baseten. Drafts, leads, post ideas and ad advice are written by AI; read them before you use them.

Only when you run an agent, Stupid sends Baseten what that agent needs:

  • Reply drafts and lead finder: the text of your recent messages and comments and the sender's first name.
  • Post ideas: your Instagram bio, follower count, your recent captions with their likes and comments, and links to your best post pictures so it can look at them.
  • Ad watcher: campaign totals such as spend, clicks and reach.

Reel director: when you run it, links to your own best Reels, with your captions and counts, go to Meta's Model API (Muse Wonder), which watches them and writes scripts. Stupid asks Meta to delete any copy of the video it made.

Ad creative pack: when you run Ad watcher, links to your own best post pictures go to Meta's Model API (SAM 3.1), which says where the product is. Stupid keeps only that box and crops the picture in your browser.

Voice notes: when a customer sends one, its audio goes once to Meta's Model API (Muse Voice Transcribe) to turn it into words. Stupid keeps only the words, as the message's text, for 30 days, and never stores the audio. Meta doesn't train on it and may keep requests for safety review.

Stupid never sends phone numbers, Meta IDs or access tokens to the AI provider. Baseten does not train on this data and keeps no copy of it (zero data retention). If you save your own AI key in Settings, the same data goes to your own provider instead, under your own account.

How long

  • Messages and comments: 30 days.
  • Agent results (drafts, leads, ideas, ad advice): 30 days.
  • Your sign-in session: until you sign out or it expires.
  • Meta access tokens: until you disconnect or delete your data.

How it is protected

Meta access tokens are encrypted at rest on Stupid's server and are never sent to your browser. If you add your own AI key in Settings, it is encrypted the same way and never shown again.

What Stupid never does

Stupid does not sell your data and does not share it for advertising.

Who can use Stupid

You must be 18 or older. Stupid is not meant for children.

Deleting your data

Choose Disconnect and delete my data in Stupid's Settings, or remove Stupid from your Facebook settings. Either way, everything Stupid holds about you is deleted. How data deletion works.
