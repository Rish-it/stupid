Privacy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Secnd helps creators and small businesses read and answer their Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp activity in one place. This page says what Secnd reads, why, where it goes and how to delete it.

What Secnd reads

Only the accounts you pick in Meta's window when you sign in, and only while they are switched on in Secnd:

  • Your name from Facebook, to greet you.
  • Messages to your Facebook Page (Messenger).
  • Instagram direct messages and comments on your posts.
  • WhatsApp messages to your business number.
  • Instagram insights: reach, followers and how your posts did.
  • Ad results: spend, clicks and reach for your ad accounts.

Why

To show them to you in plain words, and to let you answer from Secnd. When you run an agent, Secnd also uses them to draft replies, post ideas and ad advice for you to review. Nothing is sent, posted or changed until you tap.

Where it is kept

On Secnd's server. When you run an agent, the text it needs (for example the messages it drafts replies to) is sent to our AI provider, Baseten, to write the draft. It is not sent there at any other time.

How long

  • Messages and comments: 30 days.
  • Your sign-in session: until you sign out or it expires.
  • Meta access tokens: until you disconnect or delete your data.

How it is protected

Meta access tokens are encrypted at rest on Secnd's server and are never sent to your browser. If you add your own AI key in Settings, it is encrypted the same way and never shown again.

What Secnd never does

Secnd does not sell your data and does not share it for advertising.

Deleting your data

Choose Disconnect and delete my data in Secnd's Settings, or remove Secnd from your Facebook settings. Either way, everything Secnd holds about you is deleted. How data deletion works.
